# Router calibration — 4 September 2026

Stage 1 item 6, closed. Gold set: `data/router_labels.jsonl`, 73 queries, each
labelled on **both** axes — `suggested` = task type (7 classes), `department` =
WijerCo department (12 classes) — plus `certainty` (high / medium / low) and a
`note` on the hard negatives.

The set was written against the stated mandate of each department and the seven
task-type definitions, deliberately without reading `TASK_SIGNALS` or
`_DEPT_SIGNALS`, so it does not inherit the flaw in the old 38-row template
(machine-generated by the classifier it was then used to grade; 35 of 38 rows
"advisory"). Aaron reviewed and accepted every row on 4 September, which is what
makes it independent judgement rather than the system marking its own work.

## Result

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| task type | 60.3% | **89.0%** |
| department | 34.2% | **76.7%** |
| queries returning no department | 47% | 5% |
| queries routed to a real department (not `hybrid`) | 2 of 73 | 37 of 73 |

The signal maps were rewritten on a 48-row dev split and measured once on a
25-row holdout that was never looked at while writing them. **Holdout: task type
80.0%, department 84.0%.** Those are the honest generalisation figures; the 89.0
and 76.7 above include the rows the work was tuned on.

`tune_router.py` now reports 1 confident-wrong row out of 73, down from 5.

## What was actually wrong

Three defects, none of them thresholds.

**1. A substring match, not a word match.** `_heuristic_scores` tested
`if signal in query`. "research" contains "search", so every research query
scored *retrieval*. "assessment" contains "assess", so every assessment query
scored *reasoning*. Two of the most common words in this business were
misrouting on a fragment. Both matchers now use a word boundary with a small
suffix tolerance (`\bsignal\w{0,3}\b`), which keeps email/emails and
research/researcher matching while stopping search/research.

**2. The advisory signal list was full of department vocabulary.** It contained
`curriculum, course design, workshop, assessment design, linkedin post, article,
thought leadership, sector, wijerco, client, higher education, teqsa, aqf,
proposal`. Those name the subject matter, not the kind of work, so any query
about WijerCo's actual domain scored advisory whatever was being asked for. 24
of the 29 original task-type errors landed there. It is also why the old
template was 35/38 advisory: the classifier generated it, so the template
recorded the bug as the gold standard. `TASK_SIGNALS` now carries a rule at the
top of the block — a signal describes the kind of work, never the subject.

**3. The department router demanded two keyword hits.** Below two it returned
`hybrid` with no department. Real one-line requests supply one signal, so 64 of
73 queries fell through. Only `learning_design` had ever been tuned (it scored
9/9); the other eleven lists were written once and never measured. Those eleven
have been widened, `_score_dept` now uses the same gap-tolerant matcher as the
specialist selector rather than plain substrings, and the gate is now "wins
outright" instead of "scores at least two": measured on the dev split, one
unambiguous hit routes 33 of 48 at 91% precision against 16 of 48 at 88% for the
old rule. A tie still falls to hybrid, which is the safe answer.

`retention` and `student experience` were moved out of `research_intelligence`
into `student_experience_success`, which had been scoring 0 for 4 because the
research list was claiming its vocabulary.

## Thresholds: deliberately unchanged

`ROUTER_MIN_CONFIDENCE` stays at 0.45 and `ROUTER_MIN_MARGIN` at 0.15.
`tune_router.py` recommends 0.30 / 0.00, worth two more rows (91.8% against
89.0%). That is not worth taking. The gate never fires on this set anyway
(0 of 73 low-confidence defaults), so the recommendation is really "switch the
safety net off for no measured benefit on the cases it is meant to catch". It
exists for queries unlike anything in a 73-row set.

## Remaining errors

Eight task-type rows. Three are genuinely contested and were flagged as such
when the set was written — a query that both compares and recommends, a drafting
request whose real work is policy, and a permission question phrased as a
capability question. The routing gain from here is small and the risk of fitting
noise is not.

Seventeen department rows, mostly queries whose department is carried by context
a keyword cannot see ("build the institution profile ahead of the pitch" is
research work done for a sales purpose). Keyword routing has a ceiling and this
is roughly it. `ROUTER_USE_EMBEDDING` with per-label centroids is the next lever
if the number needs to go higher; it is off and does not need to be on yet.

## How to re-measure

    ROUTER_LABELS_PATH=data/router_labels.jsonl python scripts/tune_router.py

The gold set is now tracked in git (`.gitignore` carries an explicit exception
for it) because it is the only record of what correct routing looks like, and
every accuracy claim in this document is measured against it.
